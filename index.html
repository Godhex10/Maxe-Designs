Questions people ask first

Do I need to have my own footage?

Yes. These plans edit the clips you send us, so you'll need raw video of your business, product or team. Footage from a phone is fine.

What do you need from me after I pay?

Your raw video clips, your business name or logo, and a short note on what the video should promote. For Growth, add a short brief on your business and audience. For Premium, add access to your page so we can post, and a brief on your goals.

How long does it take?

Starter takes 1 day, Basic 2 days, Growth 3 to 4 days and Premium 5 days. The clock starts when we have both your payment and your footage.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with Starter if you want to see our work first. Choose Basic if you also need a flyer, and Growth if you want a plan for what to post. Pick Premium if you want a brand kit, a business video and someone to post for you.

Do you post on my page for me?

Only in the Premium plan. In the other plans you get the finished files and post them yourself.
